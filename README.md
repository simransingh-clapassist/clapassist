# ClapAssist

**The pay-once AI interview assistant for Mac and Windows, hidden from screen share on every plan.**

Website: **[clapassist.com](https://clapassist.com)**

ClapAssist is a desktop app for live interviews on Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex and Slack. It listens to the call; press Cmd/Ctrl + Enter and it shows one line to say first, then the points, from your own resume, in under a second.

## Features

- **Every round:** HR/behavioral, coding & DSA (reads the problem on your screen and gives runnable code), system design, technical and SQL
- **Hidden on every plan:** excluded from screen share and recordings; never joins the call as a bot; no Dock icon, not in Cmd+Tab, shortcuts never steal focus
- **One answer, then quiet:** hears both sides of the call and stops once you have answered
- **Recap after every interview:** what you were asked, how you answered, what to fix before the next round
- **Privacy you can check:** no analytics tracker inside the app, call audio and screen never stored, speech-to-text can run on your laptop, warns if proctoring software is running
- **Languages:** transcribes English, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, Chinese and more; answers in English, Hinglish or the chosen language
